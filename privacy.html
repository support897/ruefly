Ruefly — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Ruefly is a private dashboard for one account holder. It is operated by Ilse Placencia (hello@ilseplacencia.shop).

What the app holds

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in.
  • The content you and your workers put in: account states, findings, notes and roll-ups.
  • If you turn notifications on, a push token for your device so updates can reach it.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising. No ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • The app does not post to any social network, and it does not hold your social network passwords.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

Where it lives

Your content is stored in a private database (Supabase) and travels over encrypted connections. Only your signed-in account can read your dashboard.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications off any time in the app or your phone settings.
  • Ask for your account and all its content to be deleted: email hello@ilseplacencia.shop. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date at the top.
